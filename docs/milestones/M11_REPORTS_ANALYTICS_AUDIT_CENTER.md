# Milestone 11 — Reports, Analytics & Master Audit Center

## Objective
Build the comprehensive hospital reporting suite and administrative audit center, providing management census reports, departmental operational metrics, financial revenue analytics, and an interactive security audit trail with export capabilities conforming to `docs/PRD.md` and `docs/DATABASE.md`.

## Scope
- Operational & Clinical Census Reports:
  - Patient Volume & Registration Trends (daily registrations, gender/age demographics, active vs inactive patients).
  - OPD Clinic Footfall & Clinician Workload (appointments scheduled, attended, cancelled, no-show rate per doctor).
  - Inpatient Department (IPD) Census: Average Length of Stay (ALOS), bed occupancy percentage per ward, admission vs discharge ratios.
  - Laboratory Workload: Total tests ordered vs verified, category breakdown, average turnaround time (TAT).
  - Pharmacy Stock Reports: Daily dispensing volume, inventory stock-out risks, batches nearing expiry within 30/60/90 days.
- Financial Analytics & Revenue Reports:
  - Daily Cashier Collections Register: Breakdown by cashier, payment method (cash, credit card, UPI, insurance).
  - Departmental Revenue Attribution: Revenue generated by OPD, IPD, Diagnostic Lab, Pharmacy, and Procedures.
  - Accounts Receivable & Outstanding Balance Ageing: 0-30 days, 31-60 days, 61-90 days, >90 days overdue.
  - Approved Discounts and Refund Summaries.
- Master Security & Audit Trail Center (`/audit`):
  - Advanced search and filtering of the MongoDB Atlas `audit_logs` collection.
  - Filters by date range, actor email, action type (`LOGIN_SUCCESS`, `PATIENT_CREATE`, `INVOICE_CREATE`, `REFUND_APPROVE`), resource, and status.
  - Detailed inspection modal showing sanitized audit metadata.
  - Secure CSV / JSON export of audit logs for institutional compliance.
- Export Capabilities:
  - Download reports in formatted CSV for external spreadsheets.
  - Printable executive PDF summaries with official hospital header.

## Out of Scope
- AI-driven predictive patient admission forecasting (Phase 2 AI).
- Integration with external Business Intelligence (PowerBI / Tableau) pipelines.
- Direct regulatory tax portal filing automation.

## Prerequisites
- Milestone 01 (Auth & Audit logging foundation).
- Milestone 02 (Application Shell & Dashboard).
- Milestones 03 to 10 (Clinical and financial operational data available).

## User Workflows
1. **Daily Operational Review**: Medical Superintendent opens `/reports/census`, selects "Today", and reviews patient volume (42 OPD consultations, 6 IPD admissions, 14 discharges, Bed Occupancy: 78%).
2. **Financial Revenue Reconciliation**: Hospital Accountant accesses `/reports/financial`, filters by "Last 7 Days", reviews total collections ($28,450.00) grouped by OPD, IPD, Lab, and Pharmacy, and verifies cashier registers.
3. **Ageing Balance Inspection**: Accountant opens Outstanding Ageing Report, identifies accounts overdue >60 days, and exports a CSV for follow-up.
4. **Security Audit Review**: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) accesses `/audit`, filters by actor `admin@hms.local` and action `REFUND_APPROVE` over the past 30 days, reviews metadata, and exports an audit log verification file.

## Frontend Requirements
- **Pages**:
  - `/reports`: Master analytics directory grouped into Operational, Clinical, Financial, and Security categories.
  - `/reports/census`: Inpatient and outpatient clinical census dashboard.
  - `/reports/financial`: Revenue, collections, and outstanding balance analytics.
  - `/reports/pharmacy-inventory`: Expiry risks, consumption rates, and stock alerts.
  - `/audit`: Full-featured administrative security audit trail workstation.
- **Components**:
  - `DateRangePicker`: Quick selectors (Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, Month-to-Date, Custom Range).
  - `MetricsSummaryCard`: Key performance indicator cards with percentage comparisons.
  - `ReportDataTable`: Accessible tabular display with sortable headers and CSV export button.
  - `AuditLogDetailModal`: Inspected view showing timestamp, IP address, user agent, action, and sanitized details.
- **States**: Loading skeletons during aggregation queries, empty report view ("No records matching selected date range").

## Backend Requirements
- **Modules**: `ReportsModule` in `apps/api/src/reports/`.
- **Controllers**:
  - `ReportsController`:
    - `GET /api/v1/reports/census`: Outpatient/Inpatient volume metrics (`reports.read`).
    - `GET /api/v1/reports/financial`: Revenue, collections, and ageing metrics (`reports.financial.read`).
    - `GET /api/v1/reports/inventory`: Expiry and stock consumption analytics (`reports.read`).
  - `AuditController`:
    - `GET /api/v1/audit`: Query audit logs with pagination and filters (`audit.logs.read`).
    - `GET /api/v1/audit/export`: Export filtered audit logs as CSV (`audit.logs.read`).
- **Services**:
  - `ReportsService`: Executes efficient MongoDB aggregation pipelines (`$match`, `$group`, `$facet`) on operational collections.
  - `AuditQueryService`: Handles paginated queries on `audit_logs` collection.

## Database Requirements
- **Collections Utilized** (All Tenant-Owned): `users`, `patients`, `appointments`, `admissions`, `beds`, `lab_orders`, `dispensing_records`, `invoices`, `payments`, `audit_logs`.
- **Indexes Utilized**:
  - `audit_logs`: `{ tenantId: 1, timestamp: -1 }`, `{ tenantId: 1, action: 1, timestamp: -1 }`, `{ tenantId: 1, userId: 1, timestamp: -1 }`
  - `appointments`: `{ tenantId: 1, scheduledAt: 1, status: 1 }`
  - `admissions`: `{ tenantId: 1, admissionDate: 1, status: 1 }`
  - `invoices`: `{ tenantId: 1, createdAt: 1, status: 1 }`
  - `payments`: `{ tenantId: 1, paidAt: 1, method: 1 }`

## API Requirements
- `GET /api/v1/reports/census`: Query `{ startDate, endDate }`, scoped to `tenantId`, returns `{ success, data: CensusReport }`.
- `GET /api/v1/reports/financial`: Query `{ startDate, endDate, department? }`, scoped to `tenantId`, returns `{ success, data: FinancialReport }`.
- `GET /api/v1/audit`: Query `{ startDate?, endDate?, action?, userId?, page, limit }`, scoped to `tenantId`, returns `{ success, data: AuditLog[], meta: PaginationMeta }`.

## RBAC Requirements
- `reports.read`: `super_admin`, `hospital_admin`, `doctor` (department heads).
- `reports.financial.read`: `super_admin`, `hospital_admin`, `accountant`.
- `audit.logs.read`: `super_admin`, `hospital_admin`.

## Security Requirements
- **Tenant Isolation**: All aggregation pipelines and audit log searches strictly scoped to caller's verified `tenantId`. Aggregations never mix metrics across tenants.
- Aggregation pipelines must enforce hospital/tenant boundaries.
- Rate-limiting applied to heavy report and export endpoints to prevent resource exhaustion.
- Audit export files must never contain passwords, tokens, or encryption keys.

## Audit Requirements
- `REPORT_GENERATE`: Records report type, date range, and requesting staff ID.
- `AUDIT_EXPORT`: Records export filter parameters, record count, and requesting administrator ID.

## UX Requirements
- Instant date range presets for common operational shifts (Today, This Week, This Month).
- Clean print layout removing navigation bars and headers when printing reports.
- Fast, non-blocking asynchronous CSV generation for large datasets.

## Testing Requirements
- Unit tests:
  - Aggregation pipeline accurately calculates bed occupancy percentages.
  - Ageing bucket categorization (0-30, 31-60, 61-90, >90) allocates invoices to correct bins.
  - Audit query service applies date range and action filters correctly.
- API tests:
  - Unauthorized roles (e.g. receptionist) blocked from accessing financial and audit reports.
  - Invalid date ranges return 400 Bad Request.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Clinical census report computes patient footfall, admissions, and bed occupancy.
- [ ] Financial report aggregates collections by payment method and department.
- [ ] Accounts receivable ageing report groups outstanding balances accurately.
- [ ] Interactive audit trail allows filtering by date, user, action, and status.
- [ ] CSV export operational for reports and audit logs.
- [ ] RBAC permissions strictly enforce access to financial and audit data.
- [ ] Unit and API tests pass cleanly.

## Dependencies

## Implementation Notes
- Use MongoDB aggregation `$facet` to compute multiple metrics (e.g. totals, payment method breakdown, and hourly distribution) in a single database roundtrip.

## Do Not Implement
- Automated AI forecasting models or direct integration with third-party cloud data warehouses.
